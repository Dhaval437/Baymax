01 · Network

Choose Baymax’s Wi-Fi

Enter a 2.4 GHz network or a compatible phone hotspot. Baymax remembers it after connecting.

Changing networks can briefly disconnect your phone. Rejoin Baymax-Setup-… and reopen http://192.168.4.1. A failed connection keeps your previous settings.

02 · Conversation

Connect to your laptop

The laptop still runs Baymax’s voice and answers. Put it on the same Wi-Fi, open Start-Baymax, and copy its “Phone setup server address” here.

Wi-Fi connected does not mean the conversation server is ready. Start it on the laptop, then tap Resume Baymax and say “Baymax” or “ouch”.

Wireless firmware update

Choose a Baymax phone-setup application .bin made for this ESP32-S3. Do not use a bootloader, partition file or merged image.

Updating pauses listening and motor output. A completed update restarts the robot; an interrupted update needs a retry. Use this only on your own trusted network.

Works in your phone’s browser. No app-store install or cloud account. Internet-login pages and enterprise Wi-Fi are not supported.
